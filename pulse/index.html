Ask us a question ↗
Can Pulse work with our attendance machine?

Pulse supports attendance imports from compatible CSV or text exports. Share a sample export during the walkthrough so we can confirm the format and any mapping needed. A direct connection to every device is not assumed.

Does the system handle Sri Lankan payroll requirements?

Pulse is designed for Sri Lankan HR and payroll workflows. Applicable statutory rules, effective dates and report requirements need to be confirmed during setup and maintained through an agreed review process. Your authorised payroll reviewer remains responsible for checking the pay run.

Can we move from spreadsheets?

We start by reviewing your employee records, opening balances, salary structure and attendance files. The written setup scope will identify what can be imported, what needs cleaning and what must be checked before use.

Is payroll processing included in the subscription?

The software subscription and professional payroll processing are separate services. If you need someone to carry out or review the work, ask about CeyDesk support. Scope, responsibility and service fees are agreed separately.

Are Pulse, Orbit and CeyDesk already one login?

They have separate access routes today. The Evokes Digital Company Suite brings the products and service options together commercially. Any shared sign-in, data transfer or integration requirement must be confirmed in your proposal.

How will I know which plan is right?

We review your headcount, users, pay cycle, required features and support needs. You receive a written proposal showing the subscription, capacity, setup costs, billing terms and any separate professional services before you decide.
