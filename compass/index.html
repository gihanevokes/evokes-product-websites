Capability

Three questions, one set of records.

Every trading business asks these each week. Compass answers all three from the same shipment, instead of three spreadsheets that disagree.

What did it really cost?

Supplier invoice, freight, insurance, duties, clearing, transport and bank charges gathered against the shipment, allocated to the lines that caused them, and closed with the variance against the estimate in plain view.

Where is the shipment?

Bookings, containers, departure and arrival, declaration status and release, document and permit checklists, with alerts before free time runs out rather than after the demurrage has been charged.

What do we owe, and who owes us?

Supplier and service-provider bills, customer invoices, receipts, trade-finance commitments and export proceeds, each in its own currency with the rate and the date it was taken.

Questions

The details worth checking.

Tell us where your workflow differs. We confirm what the current release supports before anything is agreed.

Does Compass file our customs declarations?

No. Compass records the declaration, the assessed values and levies, the payment and the release, and reconciles them against your commercial documents. Filing stays with your broker. Any direct interface depends on formal access being granted, and would be confirmed in writing before it is promised.

How are duty and levy rates kept current?

They are configuration, not program code. Each rule carries its official source, its effective dates, the person who approved it and the test cases it passed, so a change takes an approval rather than a software release. A shipment declared earlier keeps the rule version that applied on its declaration date.

What happens when a freight bill arrives after we have received the goods?

Stock is received at a provisional cost so the warehouse is not held up. When the late bill is matched, the adjustment splits between stock still on hand and stock already sold, and posts as an adjustment rather than a silent restatement of a closed figure.

Does Compass replace our accounting system?

That is a decision we make with you. Compass can hold the trade subledgers and send approved postings to your existing accounting package, or carry more of the ledger itself. The choice materially affects cost and delivery time, so it is settled during the first engagement rather than assumed.

Can we use Compass with Orbit and Pulse?

Yes, they can be discussed together. Shared sign-in across the suite is in development. We do not describe any integration, shared login or combined offer as available until it is confirmed in writing.

How much will it cost?

The quote depends on the agreed scope, your shipment volume, the accounting boundary, how much data is migrated and which interfaces are needed. We do not publish a figure that would have to be withdrawn. The first engagement is priced on its own and produces documents you keep.
