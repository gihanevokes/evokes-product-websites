Ask us your question ↗
Is Orbit right for my type of business?

Orbit is intended for stock-based trading workflows involving goods received, sales, returns, customer receipts and supplier payments. We review your actual process first. Manufacturing, batch or expiry tracking, specialist retail requirements and other complex workflows need a separate fit assessment.

Can we bring our existing items and balances?

We review your item list, opening stock and customer or supplier balances before agreeing the migration scope. The opening figures need to be checked against your records and physical stock; importing a file alone does not verify the quantities.

Does taking a payment change the stock again?

A payment and a stock movement are different events. In the sample trading day, the sale reduces stock once and the later receipt reduces only the customer balance. Your actual document flow and posting rules will be checked in the product walkthrough.

Does Orbit replace a full accounting or ERP system?

Orbit focuses on inventory and trading records. Customer and supplier balances do not, by themselves, make it a complete general ledger or ERP. Tell us which accounting reports, exports or integrations you need so the scope can be confirmed.

Can we use Orbit with Pulse and CeyDesk?

Yes, you can discuss them together as part of the Evokes Digital Company Suite. They have separate access routes today. Professional services are scoped separately, and any data integration or shared sign-in requirement needs to be confirmed before commitment.

How much will the subscription cost?

The quote depends on the agreed features, users, locations, capacity and support scope. Your proposal will identify the subscription, setup or migration costs, billing terms and any separate services before you decide.
