Ask us a question
Do we need to use all three?

No. Orbit, Pulse and CeyDesk each work on their own. Most businesses start with the one that addresses their most pressing routine, and discuss the others later.

Are Orbit, Pulse and CeyDesk one login?

Not today. Orbit and Pulse have separate access routes, and CeyDesk will have its own. The suite brings the products and services together commercially. Any shared sign-in, data transfer or integration requirement must be confirmed in your proposal.

Is there a combined price for the suite?

There is no published suite price. Each product is scoped and quoted after a fit review. If a combined offer is agreed, it is confirmed in writing, and professional services are always quoted separately from software subscriptions.

When will CeyDesk be available?

CeyDesk is in preparation. We will confirm the services, availability and fees before launch. Register your interest and we will contact you when there is more to share.

Is payroll processing included in Pulse?

No. Pulse is software your team uses to prepare and review payroll. Having someone carry out or review the payroll work is a professional service, planned as part of CeyDesk and scoped and charged separately.

Who is behind the suite?

Evokes Digital Worldwide (Pvt) Ltd, a Colombo-based technology company established in 2004. Evokes offers Orbit and Pulse, and builds and operates the CeyDesk platform. Professional work through CeyDesk will be carried out by qualified service providers.
